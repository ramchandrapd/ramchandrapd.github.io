Biography

Hi, I'm Ramchandra Poudel. I created this space to document what I learn, think about, and create. My interests include technology, research, writing, productivity, and lifelong learning, along with my academic journey in agriculture. I hope these articles encourage curiosity, critical thinking, and continuous learning.

I'm currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Agriculture (2025–present) at the College of Natural Resource Management (CNRM), Puranchaur, Pokhara, Kaski. I previously completed my +2 (ISC Agriculture) in Syangja, Nepal. This website is where I document my learning journey and share ideas on topics that interest me.
